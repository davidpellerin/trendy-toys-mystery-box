What could be in your box?

These are the kinds of fidgets we pack. Think of it as the shelf we shop from — which ones land in your box is the part we're not telling you.

  • Infinity cubes

    Fold it, flip it, fold it again. The one that never quite ends.

  • Squishy friends

    Slow-rise squishies that squash flat and puff back up again.

    Pull, twist, knot, snap back. Stretches way further than it looks like it should.

    Stretchy, squishy, occasionally glittery. Sealed tub, we promise.

    Walk it down a stack of books. Yes, it still works. Yes, we tested it.

    A fresh sheet in every box, plus one bonus item we don't announce.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Where do you ship, and how long does it take?

We ship to the US and Canada, and shipping is included in every plan. Boxes go out in the first week of each month; most arrive within 3–8 business days depending on where you're located. TODO: confirm your carrier and timelines.

Can I cancel?

Every plan has a minimum term — 3, 6, or 12 months — so you're billed for the full term you choose. Manage your subscription from the link in any billing email; turn off auto-renew anytime and it simply won't renew at the end of your current term. No phone call, no retention pitch.

How does "Suggest a Toy" work?

If you're on the 6-month or 12-month plan, you'll get a link in your billing email to submit toy or fidget ideas you'd like to see in a future box. We review submissions each month when planning the next drop — it's not a guarantee any specific item shows up, but it directly shapes what we pack. 12-month subscribers get priority when we're picking from the list.

Who is the box for?

Anyone who likes fidgets — kids, teens, students, and plenty of adults who keep one on their desk. Boxes are packed with a general audience in mind rather than tailored to a specific age or need.

Is it safe for young children?

Some items contain small parts and are not suitable for children under 3. Every box is labelled accordingly. If a box is going to a young child, adult supervision is recommended. TODO: confirm this wording against the labelling on the products you're sourcing.

When am I billed?

Once, up front: one payment covers every box in your term (3, 6, or 12), and there's nothing more to pay until renewal — if you haven't turned off auto-renew by then. Order before the end of a month to receive that month's box; after that, your first box is the following month's.
